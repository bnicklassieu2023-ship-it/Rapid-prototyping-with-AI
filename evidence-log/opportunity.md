# Opportunity

## Business idea
An AI assistant that helps final-year students and recent graduates find relevant job or university opportunities based on their profile, goals, budget, location preferences, and expectations.

## User
Final-year students and recent graduates who want to continue with either employment or higher education but feel overwhelmed by the number of options.

## Need
They need a faster and more personalized way to identify which jobs or university programs fit them best.

## Pain point
Students often spend many hours searching across different websites, comparing options manually, and still feel uncertain about whether the opportunities really match their profile and goals.

## Insight
Users do not only want a list of options. They want a shortlist with reasons, fit logic, and less confusion.

## How might we
How might we help students quickly discover relevant opportunities and understand why those options fit them?

## Open gaps
- Do users prefer one assistant for both jobs and universities, or should these be separate?
- Which matters more: speed, personalization, or trust in the recommendations?
- Will users share enough personal information to get useful recommendations?

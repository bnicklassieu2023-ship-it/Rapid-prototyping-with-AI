# Persona hypothesis: The manual comparer (provisional label)

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

When they are in their final year or have recently graduated and must choose between a job and further study, [trigger: open gap] prompts final-year students and recent graduates to seek confidence about which next step fits their profile and goals, without spending many hours comparing options by hand (see evidence-log/jtbd.md)

## Behavioural archetype

A final-year student or recent graduate who looks for jobs or master's programs by searching several websites separately and comparing the options by hand, and who still feels unsure whether the options fit.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Final year or recently graduated, weighing employment against higher education. Career goal, location, budget, field, deadlines and remote or on-site preference are the inputs our test asks for. Whether these are the constraints that matter to real users is not yet known.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Searches across different websites and compares options manually.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Manual search and comparison. Any use of AI tools today is unknown.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** UNKNOWN. Not yet established by any real user.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Whether the first version must show reasons for each recommendation, including what does not fit, and whether that explanation is what earns trust. This links to the team's riskiest assumption in assumptions.md.

## Validation gaps

- Every claim above is still a team interpretation. The first real-user conversations should ask each person to describe the last time they searched for a job or master's program, step by step.
- Whether this behaviour is one pattern or two (searching for jobs versus searching for master's programs) is unknown.
- What makes a recommendation credible enough to act on is unknown.

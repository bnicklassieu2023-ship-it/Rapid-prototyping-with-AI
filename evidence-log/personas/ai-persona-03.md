# Persona 3: Daniel, The Starter

**Status:** Proto-persona, v0.2. Rebased on the United States on 1 October 2026 when the team changed market. Built from secondary research and our opportunity.md, checked against 1 real-user interview (interview-evidence.md). Not validated. The earlier European version of this persona is in the repository history.

## Shared Job to Be Done

See evidence-log/jtbd.md. Find jobs or internships that fit, without hours of searching and mass applying.

## Profile

- **Age:** 19
- **Context:** sophomore at a regional public university, first in his family to attend college, no professional network
- **Looking for:** a first part-time job or internship to get experience on a resume

## Current behaviour

Scrolls postings on his phone and gives up when they ask for experience. Has not booked a careers-office appointment because the next slot is weeks away.

## Goals

- Know where to start
- A first line on his resume
- Keep up with classmates who already have internships

## Pain points

- Every posting asks for experience
- The careers office is booked out
- Unsure whether using AI is allowed or reliable

## Motivations

Guidance, convenience, free

## Objections

- Will not pay himself
- Unsure whether AI is trustworthy

## Buying role and journey

- **Role:** user and influencer. The buyer would be the university careers office under a licence. Parents are a secondary influencer.
- **Journey:** hears about it from the careers office or a professor, tries it free with his student login, uses it monthly if it gives clear first steps.
- **Sources of information:** Instagram, TikTok, group chats, university announcements

## Quote (illustrative)

"I don't even know which jobs I'm allowed to apply for yet."

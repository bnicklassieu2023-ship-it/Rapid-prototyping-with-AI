# Persona 3: Daniel, The Starter

**Status:** Proto-persona, v0.1. Built from secondary research and our opportunity.md. Checked against 1 real-user interview (interview-evidence.md). Not validated.

## Shared Job to Be Done

See evidence-log/jtbd.md. Find jobs or internships that fit, without hours of manual searching.

## Profile

- **Age:** 19
- **Context:** 2nd-year Marketing student at a public university near Madrid, first in his family at university, no professional network
- **Looking for:** a first part-time job or first internship to gain experience

## Current behaviour

Scrolls job posts on his phone and gives up when they ask for experience. Has not booked a careers-office appointment because the waiting list is weeks long.

## Goals

- Know where to start
- Get a first line on his CV
- Keep up with classmates

## Pain points

- Every post asks for experience
- Careers office is booked for weeks
- Unsure whether using AI is allowed or reliable

## Motivations

Guidance, convenience, free

## Objections

- Will not pay himself
- Unsure whether AI is trustworthy

## Buying role and journey

- **Role:** user and influencer. The buyer would be the university careers service (a B2B2C licence). Parents are a secondary influencer.
- **Journey:** hears about it from the careers office or a lecturer, tries it free with his university login, uses it monthly if it gives clear first steps.
- **Sources of information:** Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp groups, university announcements

## Quote (illustrative)

"I don't even know which jobs I'm allowed to apply for yet."

# Persona 1: Lucía, The Mass Applier

**Status:** Proto-persona, v0.1. Built from secondary research and our opportunity.md. Checked against 1 real-user interview (interview-evidence.md). Not validated.

## Shared Job to Be Done

See evidence-log/jtbd.md. Find jobs or internships that fit, without hours of manual searching.

## Profile

- **Age:** 21
- **Context:** 3rd-year BBA at a public university in Madrid, part-time job, tight budget
- **Looking for:** a curricular internship (prácticas) for next summer

## Current behaviour

Applies to almost everything on LinkedIn, InfoJobs and the university job board. Tracks applications in a spreadsheet. Has sent about 40 applications and received very few replies. Uses ChatGPT to write cover letters, not to decide where to apply.

## Goals

- First marketing or business internship
- Fewer, better-fitting applications
- More interviews

## Pain points

- Too many irrelevant listings
- No feedback on applications
- Cannot judge which roles are a realistic fit

## Motivations

Speed, getting interviews, low cost

## Objections

- Will not pay upfront
- Thinks LinkedIn is enough
- Doubts AI understands her field

## Buying role and journey

- **Role:** user and sole decision maker. Pays €0 at first. Upgrades (a few euros a month) only if the free tier gets her interviews.
- **Journey:** hears about it on TikTok, Instagram or from classmates, compares it with LinkedIn, uses the free tier during application season, shares it in class WhatsApp groups if it works.
- **Sources of information:** career TikTok creators, LinkedIn, friends, university careers emails

## Quote (illustrative)

"I've sent 40 applications and still don't know which ones I had a chance at."

## Evidence boundary

| Claim | Evidence status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Students send many applications before landing a role | SUPPORTED | NACE 2025: about 30 applications on average, up from 16 to 17 two years earlier |
| Young people in Spain face a hard entry-level market | SUPPORTED | Eurostat, Jan 2026: under-25 unemployment 23.5% vs EU 15.1% |
| Gen Z resists paying until the free option disappoints | SUPPORTED | Öztürk 2026, Alphanumeric Journal (free-to-paid switching study) |
| Lucía's specific behaviour, tools and 40 applications | HYPOTHESIS | Team interpretation, no real user yet |
| Willingness to upgrade to a paid tier | UNKNOWN | Not tested |

## Validation gaps

- Ask real students: "Walk me through the last internship you applied for. How did you decide to apply?"
- Test whether a fit-ranked shortlist would change where they apply

## Real-user check (interview 01, n=1)

| Claim | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Sends many applications with few results | SUPPORTED BY 1 INTERVIEW | Hundreds of applications through job websites, some interviews, never employed through one |
| Needs to know where she has a real chance | CONSISTENT | He would trust a tool that shows no unsuitable jobs |
| Won't pay until it gets interviews | NOT TESTED | Payment was not asked |

**Grounding update:** the interviewee's behaviour is closest to this Persona. The product decision (a ranked shortlist) now puts accuracy first: no unsuitable jobs.

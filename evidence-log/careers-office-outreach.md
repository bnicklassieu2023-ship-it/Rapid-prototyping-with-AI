# Careers-office outreach

**Why:** our partnership funnel (market-research.md, Funnel B) assumes a US careers office will adopt a second tool alongside Handshake and pay for it. That assumption has no evidence of any kind. Two conversations would change that.

**Status:** script ready, nobody contacted yet. Owner: [team member]. Target: 2 conversations.

## Who to contact

Career services staff at US four-year institutions, reachable through IE's exchange partners, LinkedIn (Career Services Director, Associate Director of Employer Relations), alumni at US universities, and professors with US contacts.

## Email, ready to send

Subject: 15 minutes for a student research project on early-career tools

Hello [name],

I am a student at IE University in Madrid working on a university project about how students find internships and first jobs. We are not selling anything and there is nothing to buy.

We are trying to understand how a careers office decides to adopt a new tool. Would you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks for a short call? Six questions, no preparation needed, and we are happy to share what we learn across all the offices we speak to.

Thank you for considering it,
[name], IE University

## The six questions

1. What was the last new tool your office added for students, and what made you add it?
2. Who had to agree before it went live, and roughly how long did that take?
3. What does a tool have to clear before students see it, for example data protection, accessibility or security review?
4. How do students actually find out about a tool you offer, and what share of them use it?
5. What would make you add something alongside Handshake rather than seeing it as duplication?
6. Who holds the budget for this kind of tool, and what is a normal range?

## Decision rules, set before the calls

| Finding | What we do |
|---|---|
| Both offices would not add anything alongside Handshake | Funnel B is dead. Present the direct funnel only and say so |
| Procurement takes longer than a year | Keep Funnel B but push revenue to year two and state that the pilot must be free |
| Promotion reaches far fewer students than our assumed 60% | Rebuild Funnel B with their number, which moves year three from 36,000 to whatever it supports |
| At least one says yes to a pilot | The strongest single piece of evidence we could bring to the presentation |

## What we must not do

Treat a polite "interesting, send me something" as agreement. Only a named next step counts as a yes.

## What it supports

- The push force in jtbd.md: current job websites produce effort (hundreds of applications) without the result (a job).
- The Mass Applier Persona (ai-persona-01.md): high application volume, low conversion.

## What it contradicts or complicates

- The Sceptic Persona (ai-persona-02.md) assumed that a reason for every match is what earns trust. This student named accuracy, not explanations, as his condition for trust.

## Still unknown

- Whether other students share this trust condition. One interview cannot validate the Job or any Persona.
- How many unsuitable matches a student would tolerate before giving up on a tool.
- The trigger that starts a search (still open in jtbd.md).
- Willingness to share CV and personal data (not asked in this interview).
- Willingness to pay (not asked).

# Persona Agent rehearsal

**Status: SYNTHETIC REHEARSAL. This is not evidence and nothing here validates anything.** The course is explicit: synthetic Persona answers can rehearse research but cannot validate the Job or the Persona. Every finding below is a question to put to real students, not an answer from them.

**Date:** 6 October 2026
**Method:** our three selected Personas were run as synthetic Persona Agents following the course prompt now stored at `agents/persona-agent.md`, using only the content of their Persona files and the evidence log. Each answer carries the course labels: OBSERVED, REPORTED, SUPPORTED, HYPOTHESIS, UNKNOWN.
**Why:** to pressure-test assumptions we had already written down, before spending the 7 real interviews on them.

## What we asked all three

1. If a site showed you five openings and said these fit you, would you use it as your first filter?
2. Would it matter that each posting is checked as real, still open and clear about work authorisation?
3. Does it matter to you that employers cannot pay to appear in your list?
4. Would you use it if your college offered it free? Would you ever pay yourself?
5. Would you want to be told that a role you are applying for is shrinking?

## What came back, and where it pushed back

| Persona | Useful answer | Label | What it challenges |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lucía, the Mass Applier | Five results would not feel like a search. She associates a short list with a weak search, not a careful one | HYPOTHESIS | Our Reduce row assumes fewer is better. The floor at which focused becomes empty is unknown |
| Lucía | What hurt was not finding postings, it was never hearing back | HYPOTHESIS, consistent with user-feedback.md | Our Job statement is about finding fitting roles. The sharper pain may be silence after applying, which we do not address at all |
| Karim, the Sceptic | Verification matters only if it covers work authorisation. A posting verified as real but silent on sponsorship still wastes his time | HYPOTHESIS | Our Raise row promises authorisation clarity and the data spike lists that as unproven. If it fails, the Raise row loses most of its value for international students |
| Karim | He had never thought about paid placement, and removing it would not by itself make him trust us | HYPOTHESIS | Our Eliminate row is a cost and integrity move, not a persuasion lever. It should not lead the pitch |
| Daniel, the Starter | He does not know his field well enough to use a shift view, and being told his field is shrinking would discourage rather than redirect him | HYPOTHESIS | Our Create row assumes the information helps. Leading with "where else your profile fits" may matter more than the warning |
| Daniel | He would use whatever his college puts in front of him and would not pay | HYPOTHESIS, consistent with the persona | Supports the university channel, but says nothing about whether a college would adopt it |

## Where the rehearsal failed the sniff test

All three Personas eventually said they would try the product. That is the cooperation failure our course materials warn about, so we treat none of it as demand evidence. The value of this session is the five objections, not the three yeses.

## What we propose to change, pending team confirmation and real evidence

| Assumption or ERRC row | Before | Proposed after the rehearsal | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reduce: breadth far below standard | Fewer is simply better | Fewer is better up to an unknown floor; we must learn where a short list reads as empty | Question added to the interviews |
| Raise: verification depth | Verification earns trust | Verification earns trust only if it includes eligibility; without reliable authorisation data its value drops for one of our three Personas | Linked to spike-shift-monitor.md |
| Eliminate: no employer-paid placement | A trust-winning promise | Kept as a strategic and cost move, but it does not lead the pitch | Proposed |
| Create: shift monitor | Tell students their role is shrinking | Lead with adjacent roles their profile transfers to, never with a warning alone | Proposed, and consistent with what the spike says we can build |
| Job to Be Done | Find openings that fit so applications turn into interviews | Unchanged for now, but the rehearsal raises whether the sharper pain is silence after applying | Question for the real interviews |

## Questions this adds to the 7 real interviews

1. When you last searched, what hurt more: not finding roles, or not hearing back?
2. How many results would feel like a real search to you: 5, 10, 25?
3. If you need sponsorship or work authorisation, where do you check whether you can apply?
4. Would you notice an email from your careers office about a new tool?
5. If we told you a role you are applying for is shrinking, what would you actually do with that?

## Evidence boundary

Everything above was produced by an AI role-playing our own Persona files. It inherits our assumptions and cannot contradict them from outside. It is recorded so the reasoning is traceable, and so the five questions it generated can be answered by real students.

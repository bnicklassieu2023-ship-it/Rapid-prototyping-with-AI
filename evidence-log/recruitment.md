# Recruitment

**Status:** Plan for the Group Project I critical test. Owners and dates to be filled by the team.

## Who we need

| Element | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Target | Students aged 19 to 24 in Spain who looked for a job or an internship in the last 12 months |
| Must include | At least 2 who are first or second year (tests the Starter persona, which has no real-user evidence yet) |
| Exclude | Members of our own group, and anyone who has already seen our prototype |
| Numbers | 7 filmed interviews, plus a survey aiming for at least 50 responses |
| Format | Interviews filmed for the presentation, about 10 minutes, 4 to 6 questions about the last real search |
| Second test | Survey distributed in the class Teams chat and beyond our course, to check whether the interview findings hold in a wider group |

## What we offer

No payment. We offer to share the results and the shortlist tool when it exists. Interviews are short and arranged around the participant.

## Consent and recording

Before filming we say, and record the answer: what the recording is for (a university course presentation), who sees it (our class and professor), that no personal contact data is published, and that they can stop at any time. Survey responses are anonymous unless the person leaves an email for a follow-up interview.

## Sample bias we must declare

Our channels reach IE students and our own networks first. That over-represents private-university students in Madrid and under-represents the Starter persona and students outside big cities. We state this limitation on the slide rather than hiding it.

## v0.2 (1 October 2026): market moved to the United States

Our market is now the US, while our recruited participants are mostly students in Spain. The team decided to treat their behaviour as a proxy for US students. This is a declared assumption, not a finding, and we state it on the slide.

| What we add | Why |
|---|---|
| Send the survey to US contacts: exchange students, friends studying in the US, student subreddits such as r/internships | Gives at least some US responses to compare against the European ones |
| Two interviews with US careers-office staff | Tests the partnership funnel, which currently has no evidence at all |
| Report European and US answers separately when both exist | Keeps the proxy assumption visible rather than hidden in an average |

The survey question about platforms still lists InfoJobs, because the form went live before the market changed. We keep it and read it as a European-sample marker rather than editing responses after the fact.

# Goals

## Product goal
Help students and recent graduates reduce time and uncertainty when identifying the right job or university opportunities.

## Sprint goal
Learn whether users find AI-generated opportunity recommendations valuable and trustworthy enough to use as a first filter.

## Why this sprint goal matters now
If users do not trust or value the matching recommendations, then there is no point building later features such as tailored CVs, motivation letters, or auto-application workflows.

## v0.1 (Session 8): update

### Product goal
Kept. The interview does not change the venture direction: students still need less time and uncertainty to find the right opportunity. Interview 01 adds that the result that matters is getting hired.

### New sprint goal
Learn whether students trust AI-generated job and internship recommendations enough to use them as a first filter when every match fits their stated profile and interests.

### Why this sprint goal now
Our riskiest assumption is still trust. Interview 01 suggested that trust depends on accuracy (no unsuitable jobs), so the next sprint tests accuracy and first-filter use together.

### Previous sprint goal (v0)
Learn whether users find AI-generated opportunity recommendations valuable and trustworthy enough to use as a first filter.

## v0.2 (7 October 2026): the sprint goal, cut down to one thing

**This is the current sprint goal. Everything above it is the audit trail.**

### Why revised

The v0.1 sprint goal read: "Learn whether students trust AI-generated job and internship recommendations enough to use them as a first filter when every match fits their stated profile and interests." That sentence contains three things: trust, use, and accuracy. The graded feedback asked us to pick one and carry it through. A sprint goal that names three cannot be the thing a single metric answers.

### Sprint goal, v0.2

> **Learn whether students judge the openings we put in front of them as genuinely fitting.**

One claim, measured by one number, the fit rate. See [experiment-card.md](experiment-card.md), v0.2.

### Why this sprint goal now

Our riskiest assumption is accuracy, which is our trust assumption. Everything we have chosen to be different about, verification depth and the refusal of employer money, only matters if a student can tell the difference between a list that fits and a list that does not. If they cannot, our strategy is invisible and we should find that out this sprint, cheaply, before anything is built.

### Product goal

Unchanged from v0.1. Help students reduce time and uncertainty when identifying the right job or internship, with getting hired as the outcome that matters.

### What is not in this sprint

Whether students would use it, who pays, whether universities will partner, and whether the shift monitor is wanted. All four are live questions with their own tests. None of them is this sprint's goal, and none of them may be smuggled into this sprint's metric.

# Job to Be Done

**Status:** Working hypothesis, v0.1. Partly checked with 1 real-user interview (see user-feedback.md). Not validated.

## Job to Be Done statement

When they are studying or about to graduate and need a job or internship, [TRIGGER: open gap] prompts students aged 19 to 24 to seek confidence about which openings really fit their profile and interests, so that their applications turn into interviews and offers instead of hours of manual searching and applying

## What changed since v0

- **Scope:** narrowed from "job or further study" to jobs and internships, following our persona research. Master's programmes are parked, not rejected.
- **Desired progress:** now includes getting hired, not only finding options faster. Interview 01: hundreds of applications and some interviews through job websites, but never employed through one.
- **Anxiety force:** refined. Interview 01 linked trust to never being shown an unsuitable job.

## Job to Be Done components

| Lens | Element | Working definition | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|
| How the job begins | Circumstance | Student aged 19 to 24, studying or about to graduate, looking for a job or internship among many listings | Team interpretation (opportunity.md) plus persona research |
| How the job begins | Trigger | Not yet established. We do not know which moment starts the search (a deadline, a rejection, a friend's offer) | OPEN GAP |
| Progress | Desired progress | Know which openings really fit, apply with confidence, and get interviews and offers | Partly supported: interview 01 (n=1) |
| Progress | Solution hired | Job websites and applying at high volume, comparing listings by hand | Partly supported: interview 01 (hundreds of applications through job websites) |
| Forces compelling change | Push | High effort without the result: many applications, some interviews, no job through the websites | Partly supported: interview 01 (n=1); NACE 2025 (about 30 applications per role) |
| Forces compelling change | Pull | A shortlist where every job fits their profile and interests | Partly supported: interview 01 (n=1) |
| Forces opposing change | Habit | The familiar routine of searching and applying on known websites | Team interpretation, not yet checked |
| Forces opposing change | Anxiety | A new tool will show irrelevant jobs; reluctance to share personal information | Irrelevant jobs: interview 01 (n=1). Data sharing: not yet checked |

**Relevant progress dimensions:** functional (find fitting openings, get hired) and emotional (less uncertainty). Social is not yet established.

## Validation gaps

- One interview is a signal, not validation. We need 5 to 8 more students.
- The trigger is still unknown.
- Answers to interview questions 1 and 2 (platforms used, main annoyance) are on video and still need a written transcript.
- Habit strength and willingness to share CV data have not been checked with a real user.

## v0.2 (7 October 2026): what survey round 1 did to the Job

Round 1, n=4, our own form. Counts and the full read-out are in [survey.md](survey.md). Round 2 from the parallel form is still to be merged, so nothing here is settled.

### The forces table, re-marked

| Element | Status before | Status after round 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Push: high effort without the result | Partly supported by interview 01 and NACE 2025 | **CHALLENGED.** 2 of 4 sent five applications or fewer, and 2 of 4 have been hired through a platform. Only one respondent resembles interview 01 |
| Solution hired: applying at high volume | Partly supported by interview 01 | **CHALLENGED by the same counts.** High volume is not what these four did |
| Pull: a shortlist where every job fits | Partly supported by interview 01 | **SUPPORTED, consistently.** All 4 named "every job shown actually fits my profile" as a trust condition, the only option all four chose |
| Anxiety: a new tool will show irrelevant jobs | Interview 01 (n=1) | **WEAKENED in degree, not in kind.** Nobody chose 1 or 2 bad matches as their limit; all 4 chose 3 to 5 or more. The fear is real, the trigger point is further out than interview 01 implied |
| Anxiety: reluctance to share personal information | Not yet checked | **Checked, and small.** Only 1 of 4 picked privacy as a trust factor |
| Trigger | OPEN GAP | **Still an open gap.** The trigger question was cut from the short survey. It goes to the interviews |

### What this does to the Job statement

The statement still holds at its core: students want confidence about which openings really fit. What no longer holds without qualification is the clause about "hours of manual searching and applying", which describes interview 01 and one of four survey respondents, not students in general.

**The circumstance is the part that needs work.** All four respondents are bachelor students and three are 21 or under, so they sit earlier in the search than the student in interview 01. Either the Job belongs to a narrower moment than "studying or about to graduate", or the high-volume pattern appears later and we have been sampling the wrong end of it. Both readings are live. The seven interviews decide which, by asking where each student is in their search before anything else.

Until then, the circumstance row is downgraded from a team interpretation we were comfortable with to **an open question**, alongside the trigger.
